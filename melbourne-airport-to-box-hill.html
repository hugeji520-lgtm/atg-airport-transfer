Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the transfer from Melbourne Airport to Box Hill take?
Around 35–45 minutes in normal traffic via the Eastern Freeway or EastLink. Allow extra time in weekday peak hours.
What happens if my flight is delayed?
Nothing changes for you. We track your flight and your driver adjusts to the actual arrival time at no extra charge.
Is the price really fixed?
Yes. The price you're quoted covers the vehicle, GST, tolls and airport pickup fee. It does not change with traffic or time of day, except for late-night/early-morning pickups which carry a small surcharge — we'll tell you upfront.
Do you provide child seats?
Yes. The first child seat or booster is free; additional seats are $20 each. Let us know ages when booking.
Can I pay cash?
Yes — bank transfer or cash to the driver. Cash bookings require a 50% deposit to secure the booking.
Do your drivers speak Mandarin?
Yes, we have Mandarin- and Cantonese-speaking drivers — just mention your preference when booking. 我们有中文司机，预订时说明即可。
